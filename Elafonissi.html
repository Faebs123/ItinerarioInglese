ELAFONISSI BEACH

Located on the southwestern tip of Crete, Greece, Elafonissi is consistently ranked as one of the most beautiful beaches in the world. It is actually a peninsula that often breaks into two parts by water, giving the impression of a separate island.

The Famous Pink Sand

The beach’s most striking feature is its pinkish sand. This unique color is created by thousands of crushed shells, corals, and microorganisms called Foraminifera. The intensity of the pink hue varies depending on the tide, wind, and season.

Key Features

  • Shallow Lagoons: The water is very shallow and crystal clear, making it perfect for families.
  • Nature Reserve: A protected Natura 2000 site and sanctuary for the Loggerhead sea turtle.
  • The Island: Walk across the sandbar to find secluded coves and a historic lighthouse.

Logistics & Tips

  • Timing: Arrive before 10:00 AM to beat the crowds.
  • Facilities: Basic snacks and restrooms are available near the parking area.
  • Drive: About 1.5 to 2 hours from Chania through the scenic Topolia Gorge.

Note: It is strictly forbidden to remove any sand, shells, or plants. Local authorities do perform checks!
