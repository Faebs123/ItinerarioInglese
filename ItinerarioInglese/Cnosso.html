THE PALACE OF KNOSSOS

Explore the Heart of Minoan Crete

"The Charging Bull fresco at the North Entrance, a symbol of Minoan power and ritual."

The Labyrinth of King Minos

Knossos is the largest Bronze Age archaeological site on Crete and is considered Europe's oldest city. It is the legendary home of the Minotaur and the Labyrinth.

Top Sights to See

  • Throne Room: Visit the oldest throne in Europe, flanked by Griffin frescoes.
  • Grand Staircase: A masterpiece of Minoan engineering and light-well design.
  • Royal Apartments: Explore the Queen's Megaron with its famous dolphin frescoes.
