SPINALONGA ISLAND

A Bastion of Memory and Resilience

"The abandoned village and Venetian walls, surrounded by the crystal-clear waters of Elounda."

The Island of the Living Dead

Spinalonga has a complex history, serving as a Venetian fortress, an Ottoman outpost, and most famously, a Leper Colony until 1957.

Visitor Information

  • The Main Street: Walk past the old shops and houses where the leper community once lived.
  • Venetian Fortifications: Hike the perimeter of the island to see the massive defensive walls.
  • Access: Reached by boat from the villages of Plaka, Elounda, or Agios Nikolaos.
