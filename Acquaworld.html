AQUAWORLD AQUARIUM

Located in Hersonissos on the northern coast of Crete, the Aquaworld Aquarium & Reptile Rescue Centre is one of the island’s most popular family attractions. This unique center is dedicated to protecting and caring for local marine life and rescued reptiles.

The Marine & Reptile Experience

The aquarium’s most fascinating feature is the opportunity to see and interact with rescued animals up close. Many of the reptiles and sea creatures housed here were injured, abandoned, or unwanted pets that found a safe home and a second chance at Aquaworld.

Key Features

  • Local Marine Species: Discover Mediterranean fish, octopuses, seahorses, and other sea creatures native to Greek waters.
  • Reptile Rescue Center: Meet friendly snakes, lizards, and turtles, with supervised handling experiences available.
  • Educational Visits: Staff members provide interesting facts and conservation information about the animals and their habitats.

Logistics & Tips

  • Timing: Visit in the morning for a quieter experience, especially during the summer season.
  • Facilities: A small gift shop, refreshments, and shaded resting areas are available on-site.
  • Location: Easily reachable on foot from central Hersonissos and about 30 minutes by car from Heraklion.

Note: Flash photography is discouraged in certain areas to avoid disturbing the animals.
