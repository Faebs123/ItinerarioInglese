AQUAWORLD AQUARIUM

Journey into the Blue Abyss

"The 360-degree Ocean Tunnel offers a rare glimpse into the predatory grace of the Sand Tiger Shark."

Interactive Marine Life

Acquaworld is Crete's first aquarium, known for its hands-on approach to marine education. It focuses on local Mediterranean sea life and rescued reptiles.

Key Highlights

  • The Ocean Tunnel: Walk through a glass corridor surrounded by sharks and rays.
  • Reptile Rescue: Meet rescued snakes and lizards, many of which can be safely held under supervision.
  • Touch Pools: A safe way for children to interact with starfish and sea urchins.
