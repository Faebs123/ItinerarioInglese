THE FORTEZZA OF RETHYMNO

Venetian Bastion of the Cretan Sea

"The star-shaped citadel, built by the Venetians in the 16th century to defend the city from Ottoman invasion."

A Sentinel Over the Harbor

Built on "Paleokastro" hill, this fortress is one of the best-preserved Venetian fortifications in the Mediterranean, offering panoramic views of Rethymno city.

Inside the Walls

  • Sultan Ibrahim Mosque: Originally a Venetian cathedral, later converted with an impressive dome.
  • The Bastions: Explore the heavy artillery emplacements and the underground storehouses.
  • Erofili Theatre: An open-air theatre that hosts cultural festivals during the summer.

Experience & Biodiversity

Beyond the pink shoreline, Elafonissi offers a unique ecosystem and activities for every kind of traveler:

  • Kitesurfing: The windy side of the lagoon is a hotspot for water sports enthusiasts.
  • Endemic Flora: Look for the rare Androcymbium rechingeri (winter flowering bulb) and the sea lilies that bloom in late summer.
  • The Monastery: On the way back, stop at Chrysoskalitissa Monastery, perched on a high rock overlooking the sea.
